Most development agencies prefer simple commercial contracts with private companies. Government tenders, public procurement, and institutional projects involve more paperwork, stricter requirements, and longer payment cycles — so many agencies simply avoid them.

We don't. We actively work with public sector clients and understand what it takes to participate in and win government contracts for software development.

What we offer for public procurement projects

  • Full package of closing documents — acts, invoices, technical specifications, acceptance certificates
  • Official contracts that meet public procurement requirements
  • Work under tender documentation and technical requirements (TOR)
  • Flexible payment terms — including post-payment after delivery
  • Support at all stages: from bid preparation to project handover

Why work without upfront payment?

Public institutions and government bodies often operate on strict budget cycles. Payment comes after the work is accepted and approved — sometimes weeks or months after delivery. We understand this reality and can structure contracts accordingly.

For established public sector clients with clear project scope, we are open to starting work without full upfront payment. Each case is evaluated individually based on project size, client profile, and timeline.

What types of projects we handle

  • Internal management systems and portals for government agencies
  • Redmine installation, configuration and migration for public sector organisations
  • CRM and ERP systems for municipal and state institutions
  • Custom Ruby on Rails applications under technical specifications
  • Modernisation of legacy government software

Documentation we provide

We prepare all documents required for public procurement acceptance:

  • Service agreement / contract
  • Technical specification (TOR)
  • Work completion act
  • Invoice for payment
  • Software handover documentation
  • User and technical manuals on request

How to start

If you have a tender, a technical specification, or simply a project that requires formal documentation — contact us. We'll review the requirements and tell you honestly whether we can participate and on what terms.

We work with clients across Europe and are experienced with cross-border procurement documentation.
